# F&B Trend Report — May 15, 2026

## Executive Summary
- **High-protein & health-halo content dominates** — cottage cheese everything, yogurt bowls with chocolate shells, and 2-ingredient onion chips continue driving massive engagement across TikTok and Instagram.
- **Comfort food elevated** — viral remakes of kebabs, Philly cheesesteaks, baked feta 2.0 (honey + chili), and charcuterie nachos are the top-performing recipe formats.
- **ASMR + food is a proven engagement hack** — live scraping data shows top F&B posts average 4-39M plays with strong ASMR/mukbang crossover appeal.
- **Low-effort, visually dramatic hacks win** — no-peel eggs, fondant potatoes, air fryer pasta chips, and dumpling bakes all trended hard due to their satisfying "process" visuals.
- **Instagram leans aesthetic (chaos cakes, butter boards, smoothie bowls), TikTok leans visceral (satisfying hacks, mukbang, first reactions).**

## Top Content Patterns (from scraping + web research)

### Pattern 1: High-Protein Everything
The biggest macro trend. Creators are finding ways to inject protein into every meal — cottage cheese pizza crusts, Greek yogurt ice cream, cottage cheese queso, protein-packed breakfast bowls. The #highprotein hashtag is surging across both platforms.

### Pattern 2: 2-3 Ingredient Magic
Recipes requiring absurdly few ingredients drive outsized engagement. 2-ingredient onion chips (onion + parmesan), 2-ingredient cottage cheese ice cream, and 3-ingredient dumpling bakes consistently outperform more complex recipes.

### Pattern 3: Satisfying Process / Food ASMR
From fridge restocks to cheese-cutting close-ups to rolling doner kebab logs, videos that emphasize tactile, auditory satisfaction get shared aggressively. Top scraped post: 4.7M likes on a spam musubi + chapagetti mukbang.

### Pattern 4: Low-Effort Comfort Food Hacks
Recipes that make restaurant-style food achievable at home with shortcuts — no-spit doner kebab, baked feta with honey + chili, Trader Joe's shortcut dumpling bake, onion boil. These get massive save-and-try-later rates.

### Pattern 5: Visual Spectacle & Whimsy
Chaos cakes (45%+ engagement surge), butter boards, Dubai-style chocolate (pistachio + kataifi), and cross-hatched vegetables. These are made for Instagram's aesthetic-first audience but cross over to TikTok well.

### Pattern 6: AI & Humor in Brand Content
Brands like Second Cup (Canada) used ChatGPT-generated fake menu items as an April Fool's prank, blending AI novelty with playful brand identity. The "absurd but real" format resonates with younger demographics.

## Content Angle Recommendations

1. **"High Protein, Low Effort" Series** — Show 2-3 ingredient high-protein recipes. This is the #1 growth angle right now. Focus on cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, and egg-based hacks. Demo in 30-60 seconds. Include a "surprisingly good" taste-test reaction.

2. **Food ASMR + Satisfying Process** — Do close-up, high-audio-quality prep videos: cheese pulls, sauce drizzles, crispy breaks, dough stretching. Scraped data shows ASMR food posts average 49.9K views/post — highest engagement per post of any food category.

3. **"Restaurant Hack" Shortcuts** — Show how to recreate viral restaurant/fast-food items at home in under 10 minutes. Doner kebab hack, Philly cheesesteak at home, Dubai chocolate bar DIY. These get high save/share rates.

4. **Seasonal Limited-Edition Content** — Tie content to seasonal moments (summer = smoothie bowls, fruit-based desserts; fall = baked goods, comfort foods). Use trending sounds and participate in platform challenges.

5. **Behind-the-Scenes + Brand Storytelling** — Content that shows "how it's made" — factory tours, test kitchen fails, packaging reveals. Audiences on TikTok value raw authenticity over polished production. Show the mess, the win, the personality.

6. **Interactive & Polarizing Content** — "Try this or that" taste tests, "rate my meal" gamification, controversial food takes. Content that drives comments and debate gets algorithm favor. Second Cup's fake AI menu prank is a perfect template.

7. **Cross-Platform Repurposing** — Shoot vertical video optimized for TikTok, repost to Instagram Reels with platform-appropriate hashtags. Add platform-specific hooks. The same recipe video works on both if you change the text overlay and hashtag strategy.
